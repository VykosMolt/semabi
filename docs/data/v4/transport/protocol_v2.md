V1's source, original initial traces and failed preparation are preserved at
local commit `795d073`. It had encoded an unobserved pre-reset state as an empty
Observation, which the frozen parser rejected at node zero. No held-out outcome
was opened before this correction. V2 corrects initialization, adds a genuine

Preregistered before fresh evaluation outcomes. The immutable freeze manifest,

## Scope and hypotheses

The learner and structural policies are the clean reviewed implementation
`9bc371ce9af65241426b77585aa79352bdd7f123`. Learning/refitting by that algorithm is
allowed; no production repair is part of T1. The instrument is a new, separately
frozen composition of existing mechanisms. This measures recovery of
representations by the same algorithm on new interfaces, not direct application
of Harbour's pinned representation and not transfer to independently authored
real applications. Fixture construction is delegated with a fresh context; that
agent receives the scientific design brief and public interface restrictions.

- H1, representation: on each of two materially different generated interfaces,
  the fitted model recovers the acted-on object and relevant related resource,
  including their displayed quantitative values. Independent state/argument
  checks must corroborate the bindings. Outcome accuracy alone cannot pass H1.
- H2, expressible relations: with eligible evidence and bindings, at least one
  comparison between bound quantities is learned and generalizes to held-out
  combinations that distinguish it from constants or independent thresholds.
  Representational, field-policy and evidence failures are separately diagnosed.
- H3, acquisition: at equal primitive budgets, the contested treatment removes
  all support for at least one rival outcome on the common evaluation surface
  that the untargeted control retains, without introducing a new forced-wrong
  outcome. This is a modest paired descriptive criterion, not a significance
  claim. Discovery of additional behavior may widen justified uncertainty and
  must be reported even when H3 is falsified.
- H4, ambiguity: where independently specified alternatives are observationally
  equivalent under the permitted interface, no unique semantic identification is
  warranted. An unsuccessful search alone cannot establish this equivalence.

The fixture author specifies hidden semantics and withheld cases independently.
Public workflow: dispatch cards -> detail -> carrier chooser -> check; workshop
category -> job -> station radio chooser -> attach -> start. Their structures,
object views and action sequences differ from Harbour. A reserved third
interface is withheld from this campaign and from repair design.

Fixture preflight correction: the author's v1 review task revealed an unknown
hidden bit when invoked, so it was resolvable and failed H4's design criterion.
That source and audit are retained as rejected before any learner evaluation.
V2 uses visible immutable review operands and independently specified rules that
agree over the whole reachable interface; the author's transition argument and
paired complete-history checks are sealed. Ordinary initial scripts/operands
remain unchanged. Raw quantity trajectories qualify dispatch for the monotone
attack (two rises/no falls per job), but not workshop; whether the model actually
recovers those quantities is a separate measured condition.

## Evidence eligibility and controls

Initial scripts are independently supplied demonstrations of public workflows,
not policy hints. This limits claims to transfer after demonstrations. Both arms
receive byte-identical raw initial evidence. Initial outcomes may be collected
and audited before evaluation; the learner implementation will remain unchanged.
For a comparison to be eligible, both operands must vary on both outcome sides
and relevant initial outcomes must have at least two occasions. Eligibility of
the task's intended relation is distinguished from whether the learner's current
representation and structural policies actually make it available. True JOIN of
non-unique relational paths and aggregation are outside this initial language.

Run both arms on both fixtures with deterministic seeds 1701 and 1702. The
experiment runner fixes `PYTHONHASHSEED=0` and numeric-library thread counts to one.
The control uses existing `Explorer.choose` without model input. Treatment selects
the first visible button in snapshot order whose existing RULE admissible set
is contested by a nonempty decision-list guard (`v4_acquire.contested`), once per
(control, query-literal set); otherwise it uses the same Explorer fallback.
Explorer novelty counts include every selected action in both arms. No fixture
labels, oracle state, planned held-out actions or hidden IDs enter selection.
This is not claimed to be the old target-parameterized `acquire()` unchanged.

A synthetic collector-to-real-learner preflight exposed a frozen implementation
failure: the inferred Abstractor's graph and its hypotheses' graph can differ,
and a newly observed signature is added to only one before parsing. This is a
learner limitation to measure, not a reason to repair it during T1. Each failed
button recognition records its node, observed state, exception and traceback;
it supplies no contested prediction. Available recognized candidates remain
eligible, and otherwise the same Explorer fallback acts. A failed scheduled fit
records its full error and makes the model unavailable until the next scheduled
fit; it does not reuse a stale model or end the primitive budget. Fits are
attempted on the same schedule in both arms. Infrastructure/browser exceptions
outside these learner calls still terminate and preserve the partial run.

Each acquisition arm has exactly 60 attempted primitives, including its initial
reset and genuine observed boundary reload. Click, type, select, press, reload
and reset all cost one, whether they
succeed or fail. Initial scripts have their own full denominators. Reset includes
its page load; no bootstrap page is observed before reset, so a previous session
cannot leak into the next arm. The first reset's `before=null`, actual observed
after-state and result remain in the charged event ledger; it is not a paired
EvidenceLog Step because no pre-state was observed. A genuine charged reload
immediately follows and supplies the existing schema/inducer temporal boundary
with real before/after observations. Subsequent observed case resets are ordinary
Steps. Thus each run has one fewer paired Step than charged attempts, reported
explicitly. Observation calls
and settling are reported. No uncounted replay or oracle setup is available during acquisition.
The active policy does not use a special navigation planner: failure to reach a
discriminator within this allowance is an acquisition failure, not proof none
exists. Refit using the whole permitted history at 15, 30 and 45 attempted
primitives; the terminal scoring fit includes all collected paired transitions.
Reset supplies only a fresh
application state, not its contents, and is recorded in the evidence lineage.

Acquisition and evaluation use separate invocations. Evaluation scripts remain
under the fixture author's oracle directory until the algorithm, instruments and
protocol are hashed. No acquisition process opens them. Both arms are scored on
the same complete evaluation trace; those outcomes never refit any T1 model.
Evaluation is run once per fixture, preserving all failed setup/target attempts.
If the instrument is invalid, preserve its run and diagnosis, version its
correction, and name exactly which outcomes had already been exposed.

## Measurements and interpretation

Report point decision-list predictions separately from corroborated RULE and
LIST admissibility. Retain raw verdicts and summarize forced correct, forced
wrong, ambiguous and unestablished with denominators; preserve sole-vocabulary
qualifications, ambiguous-with-outcome-missing, no-model, no-channel and failed
attempts separately. Frame and argument accuracy remain distinguishable. Never
assume the observed event vocabulary is closed.

Evaluation scores each attempted click separately through the unchanged learner.
Each state, decision-list, RULE, LIST and query exception is retained with a
traceback. Emission failures are `NO_MODEL` / unestablished `runtime_failure` in
their full click denominators. Failed fits likewise retain every opportunity
for that reading. State failures contribute no fabricated claims, are counted
against the fixed attempted-click scope, and remain visible beside shared-surface
coverage. Successful state rows retain per-step native summaries; cached parsing
may continue within the model but no graph repair or evaluation refit is allowed.
The scoreboard includes failed readings with empty surfaces, so failure cannot
silently remove an inconvenient candidate from the intersection. Interrupts and
evidence/freeze-integrity violations remain fatal with partial results preserved.

Generate bounded identity candidates from initial evidence only using the
existing source-candidate algorithm (cap 8, omissions and structural restrictions
recorded). Reuse the shared-surface identity scoreboard. Report candidate-set
size, fixed evaluation action scope, node-keyed intersection and union sizes,
supported coverage, contradictions and unshared claims. Report the newly inferred
reading separately; adding it may not silently shrink the frozen-candidate
surface. Empty shared surfaces establish nothing. This scoreboard is conditional
on the candidate language, not an independent identity oracle.

Retain raw observations, actions, results, exact source/data hashes, models,
role/binding details, adopted fields/pairs/clocks, all training event masks and
literal vocabulary, evaluation query literals and representative vouches. These
allow reconstruction of complete rival support before/after, rather than merely
counting eliminated short clauses. Compare removed/surviving rival OUTCOMES and
their supporting conjunctions on the same evaluation states. Changes in learned
representation/literal language must be visible when comparing support.

Failure analysis uses independently specified oracle semantics only after the
frozen result is saved. Oracle-supplied identity/state controls, disclosed replay
or purposeful interventions are diagnostic development evidence and must be
labelled. Separate missing observations, wrong representation/binding, unavailable
language, inadequate evidence, acquisition/revision failure and observational
equivalence. Controls may identify more than one cause. A unique survivor, claim
volume, unchanged response predictions or shrinking applicability is insufficient
to establish identity.

## Preservation and continuation

All raw and first-pass results are immutable by run identity. A manifest binds
source, protocol, public contract, sealed fixture files, initial evidence and
measurement code before evaluation. Logs record process ownership, working
directory, command, times and termination. Small instruments are single-threaded;
aggregate heavy work <=12 cores and authentication/cache mutations serialized.
No existing retained artifact is regenerated by this campaign.

The manifest's `files` section contains only learner/runtime implementation and
permitted initial evidence. Its separate `sealed_evaluator_files` section is
verified by the evaluator/orchestrator; acquisition and candidate preparation
must never open those files even for hashing. Otherwise a byte-integrity check
would violate the process boundary it purported to protect. Sealed metadata
contains paths and digests only, not task values or outcomes.

T1 closes only after complete results, exposure, controls and failure analysis
are reproducible and reviewed. Then immediately choose the smallest general
repair of the most valuable demonstrated limitation. Evaluation cases used for
repair become development data. Run focused tests, retained application and
allocation/pilot/separating regressions, full integration at semantic checkpoints,
and assess reserved or newly frozen transport. Continue next toward relational
composition with identity-oracle and fully inferred conditions separated;
aggregation receives its own language and protocol when justified.
