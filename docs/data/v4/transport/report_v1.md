# T1: frozen learning algorithm on fresh generated workflows

The frozen system did not establish representation transport or learn the
intended comparisons on these two interfaces. Every registered task remains
unestablished under RULE and LIST. The contested treatment made no targeted
intervention and produced exactly the same action/observation history as its
untargeted control in all four seed pairs. This is a preserved negative result,
with a demonstrated runtime defect and additional representation/binding losses.
It does not show that the interfaces lack discriminators or that the comparison
language is intrinsically insufficient.

## What is fresh and what the fixtures distinguish

This measures the same learning algorithm after supplied demonstrations on two
generated interfaces. It does not apply the old Harbour reading unchanged.
Dispatch uses cards, details and an immediate carrier-selection dialog; workshop
uses category/job navigation and radio selection followed by attachment. Both
share backend machinery and much of the detail layout. They are materially
different from Harbour, but not independently authored applications or a broad
sample of interface architectures.

The [independent fixture review](fixture_review_v2.md) was sealed until first-pass
preservation. It confirms ordinary capacity >= demand comparisons with varying
operands on both outcome sides: dispatch has 6 positive/5 negative initial
occasions, workshop 5/4. Held-out cases distinguish the relation from a constant
outcome, a demand-only rule and a single conjunction of independent thresholds.
They do not exclude arbitrary disjunctions or lookup tables. Evaluation changes
numeric quantities/capacities on fixed familiar identities, so it is a numeric
holdout, not held-out identity generalization. Capacities cannot be edited by
acquisition; the evaluation reset supplies new capacities. Dispatch alone has
eligible raw within-episode monotone trajectories. These are raw design facts;
actual object/field recovery is a separate requirement.

The review task has two genuinely different rules that agree over its entire
immutable reachable operand set. Non-review operations do not depend on the
hidden selector, and review transitions/terminal states remain equal; induction
over permitted histories establishes observational equivalence. Unreachable
operand pairs distinguish the rules. The rejected V1 hidden-bit construction and
its diagnosis remain archived. Correct prediction on the reachable domain would
still not identify which rule is implemented. Initial demonstrations contain no
review outcomes, so unestablished review predictions are not themselves evidence
that the learner preserved the intended pair of rival semantics.

The fixture's float input conversion does not implement arbitrary-precision
decimal semantics. The frozen integer/half-unit cases are unaffected. Application
and oracle were independently expressed by one authoring agent in the same
session; the additional reviewer supplies an independent audit with recorded
exposure, not an independent application implementation.

## Independent controls and demonstrated causes

The [binding control](controls/README.md) was specified before seeing first-pass
models, then run after `8dc28ba`. It supplies oracle labels/pre-state values only
to a separate evaluator. It does not fit or modify a model. Its source does not
support the reserved interface. Per-field raw anchors, exact values, missing
bindings and optional explicitly supplied trajectory alignment remain visible.

For every reading and all five stages, raw target alignment, task controls and
visible state fidelity pass: dispatch 10/10, workshop 8/8. Corroborated job owners
and bound jobs are 0/10 and 0/8. Bound resources, relevant quantity/capacity fields,
explicit resource references and intended comparisons are 0/8 dispatch ordinary
tasks and 0/6 workshop ordinary tasks. Review fields are unavailable on both
review targets per fixture. These failures remain in their fixed denominators;
absence of correspondence is reported as unavailable, not an invented mismatch.

The causes established so far are distinct:

1. **Runtime failure on new observations.** Search deepcopies hypotheses and
   their graph, then V4 construction pairs the chosen hypotheses with the earlier
   graph. `A.ensure` adds a new signature to `A.G`; `H.parse_units` reads `H.G`
   and raises KeyError. Real synthetic integration and preserved evaluation
   tracebacks reproduce this. The fixed initial-reading branch is a useful
   graph-coherent diagnostic control, although its identity choices differ.
2. **Unavailable representation/binding.** Even graph-coherent fixed readings
   supply no corroborated task job/resource bindings. Saved queries show job
   quantities in generic view state, resource-like objects keyed by field labels,
   and absent action owners/roles. Correct raw observations rule out missing
   rendered operands as the explanation for these tested states. This finding
   does not prove every latent name or identity is wrong.
3. **Acquisition failure and incomplete observations.** Runtime recognition
   fails repeatedly, no contested intervention is selected, and the existing
   text-token policy fails on numeric inputs. Unsuccessful exploration does not
   prove a discriminator is absent. Initial review-event evidence is absent;
   later vocabulary discovery remains distinct from rival elimination.
4. **Genuine observational equivalence.** The review rule alternatives are
   equivalent by the independent fixture transition argument. The learner's
   unavailable predictions do not demonstrate that it learned and retained those
   two alternatives.

The intended numeric relation is expressible with correct bound scalar fields.
H2 was blocked before those fields and roles became available; this does not
establish a limitation of the comparison language. The raw monotone trajectory
does not license blaming the clock rule while the relevant field is unrecovered.
Missing live section normalization and a separately noticed possible suffix
statistics leak in `_normalise_sections` remain hypotheses for controlled tests,
not established explanations of the T1 graph failure. T1's full-training fit and
separately attached evaluation avoid that particular suffix-statistics path.

## Research disposition and next executable checkpoint

H1's recovery criterion is not met on either fixture. H2 is blocked by
representation/binding and runtime failures. H3's paired advantage criterion is
not met. H4's fixture non-identifiability is established; learner preservation of
its intended semantic rival pair is not demonstrated. The first-pass evidence
remains immutable regardless of later improvement.

G1 will make the two V4 construction sites carry the selected hypothesis's own
graph: `G = H.G` after final selection and `V4Abstractor(Hx.G, Hx)` in search's
candidate builder. This avoids rebinding a graph after caches/types have been
derived and preserves candidate isolation. It changes no identity doctrine,
field rule, acquisition policy, normalization or V2 implementation. Acceptance
requires actual inferred compilation to read unseen observations through parsing,
control recognition and abstraction, while frozen statistics/emissions/controls
remain fixed and source/sibling graphs remain isolated. Ordinary, promoted,
pinned and legacy-identity branches need relevant checks.

Then evaluate the repair on these disclosed development histories, run retained
and dedicated-corpus regressions and required full checks, and measure separately
reserved transport under a new freeze. Runtime availability is not enough to
claim representation recovery. Investigate the remaining field/view/binding
blocker with controlled evidence next, then proceed to non-unique relational
composition with supplied-identity and fully inferred conditions separated.
P15's conditional base and automatic structural link/union revision remain open;
aggregation is a separate competence question. This report is a checkpoint,
not the end of the authorized research and engineering.
