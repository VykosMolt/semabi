# J1 normal-learner relational composition protocol

This protocol fixes the first learned assessment before opening J1 application,
case or outcome contents. It specializes `../join_design_v1.md` and the reviewed
`live_contract_v1.md`. Root has public fixture counts, operating requirements,
source audits and disclosed T1 instrument evidence. The separately authored
fixture, reference, allocation and invariance copy are already frozen. Generated
fixture transfer and independent real-application transfer remain separate claims.

## Question, hypotheses and planned opportunities

Can the normal learner recover a relation between the action's source and the
selected target through one or more visible intermediate records, then predict
held-out endpoint combinations and a separately permuted presentation? The task
is existential relational composition. It does not ask for aggregation or exact
witness counting. The source language can evaluate a supplied conjunction over
already supplied variables; that fact does not establish that normal induction
introduces an intermediate variable or learns its referring query.

The public allocation contains four sources, four targets and eight bridges.
Training, primary held-out evaluation and separate invariance evaluation each
contain 24 designated target attempts. Each split has 24 public resets and 288
scripted primitives. The retained observed-reload boundary gives expected totals
of 313 charged attempts, 312 paired steps and one unpaired reset per split.
There are no extra calibration, navigation, repair or acquisition actions.
Counts are predictions until the actual Recorder establishes them. Failed setup,
unresolved targets, action errors and unpaired resets remain charged. An
interrupted collection retains missing positions against the fixed allocation.

The frozen rewiring contrasts include zero-, one- and two-witness cases with
the same endpoints and unchanged unary attributes, degrees and object counts.
Endpoint-pair memory predicts the same answer across those rewired states.
Independent unary conditions and shared spelling also fail to track the changed
incidence relation. Requiring a unique witness rejects the two-witness positives.
The held-out allocation uses endpoint combinations absent from training action
outcomes; existing component edges and paths exposed during setup are reported
separately. The invariance split consistently renames identities and permutes
member order without changing relation semantics. Exact cases and answers remain
in the previously frozen evaluator allocation.

Competing source-grounded hypotheses are recorded before execution:

1. The normal path supplies adequate objects/references, introduces the needed
   variables and learns a condition that survives all rewiring and invariance
   cases, with correct endpoint binding and literal output arguments.
2. Missing or merged units, wrong field/reference interpretation, or failed
   owner/selection binding prevent a relational question from being represented.
3. Adequate representation reaches native induction, but no intermediate is
   introduced, or the referring language cannot intersect the individually
   plural inverse relations. This is a hypothesis-language or proposal boundary.
4. A relevant candidate is actually present in the searched grammar but the
   evidence admits rivals, or a bounded search fails to retain an evidence-
   separated candidate. Saved candidates/evidence must establish this distinction.
5. Apparent outcome success depends on memorized identities, unary coincidences,
   stale status text or missing visibility; the paired contrasts or independent
   state/argument controls falsify a composition claim.

## Source and intervention boundaries

The baseline includes G1, G2 and the independently reviewed B1 incomplete-binding
repair. B1 is integrated at `d72335b`; its native/test bytes match the candidate
that passed 621 full-suite tests and all five dedicated corpora. The actual
implementation freeze records the precise later documentation checkpoint HEAD
and native hashes. R1 remains the earlier preserved G2 measurement.

The primary first pass uses one fully inferred native Fit. No reading, identity,
formula, intermediate parameter, output vocabulary, oracle state or desired query
is supplied. Only the frozen raw training observations and completed Steps enter
the fit. Native defaults and FROZEN_PREFIX apply at the actual paired-step count.
No evaluation Step is appended, loaded or used to refit this resident model.
The observer's primary-step selector is empty in the learner process; evaluator
task labels are joined to the saved native trace later by actual raw step IDs.

Identity and representation assistance are conditional diagnostic interventions
after preservation of this complete first pass. Each requires a separate reviewed
adapter and source/input commitment. O-ID may only repair equivalence of already
represented mentions. O-REP can supply a complete independently mapped visible
representation and is labeled accordingly. A supplied formula or wanted variable
does not become a learned result. The earlier eight-object supplied-formula
control remains evidence about native semantics only.

Collection uses unchanged Browser/Recorder bodies through the accepted generic
scope adapter. Only the evaluator actor sees scripts, cases, profile labels,
reset routing and public ancestor scopes. The predictor accepts the exact
public Observation and resolved ordinary Primitive, with its descriptor derived
from the observed node. Unknown target descriptors are omitted on resolution
failure. The frozen script proceeds independently of prediction contents.

## Phase order and process ownership

The already frozen fixture inventory is authenticated opaquely by the evaluator.
Source and collector commitments precede training collection. Raw training is
preserved with complete charged/paired accounting before the predictor freeze.
The predictor, actor, scorer, custody checks and this protocol are reviewed and
frozen before either evaluation starts. Training availability cannot authorize
a learner-policy change based on an evaluation result.

The source owner first validates the trace observer and resident model monitor
on disclosed T1 data. A real IPC pilot exercises a training page and a disclosed
unseen page, durable receipts, native forecast paths, unchanged learned content
and termination. Failed instruments, partial runs and corrections keep distinct
identities. No J1 case is a pilot input.

The primary-profile service runs on `127.0.0.1:8771` with the same `/join` and
`/reset` routes throughout. Training collection finishes before the sole fit.
The resident predictor then serves all charged primary-evaluation opportunities.
A checkpoint follows that profile. The owned primary service is stopped and
reaped, the permuted service starts at the same address, and the same predictor
and native Fit serve the invariance split. Another checkpoint follows, then a
durable shutdown receipt and reaping of both owned processes. No profile name
or script payload enters the predictor protocol.

Every action waits for a ledger fsync, authenticated acknowledgement and actor
receipt fsync. Native decision and Step associations are reconciled afterward.
An incomplete copying or custody instrument prevents its dependent action;
ordinary native prediction errors and unresolved actions remain explicit
opportunities. Any infrastructure failure retains the partial allocation.
Native `complete` measures zero action failures and is not substituted for
complete execution/accounting. The actor, scoped collector, native run and
global ledger checks are all required separately.

The user-authorized allowance is all 24 logical CPUs, normal priority, one
numerical thread per native job and hash seed zero. This supersedes historical
single-core/nice commands in the immutable fixture requirements. Assign concrete
CPUs in each phase plan. Shared fixture state remains serialized. All jobs use
the existing owned-job recorder, exclusive output identities, exact source/input
versions and explicit reaps. No external deployment, paid compute or push.

## Frozen outcome measurement

The evaluator scores saved forecasts against raw poststates. It does not fit,
call a pre-action model query retrospectively or infer a missing forecast from
the action's outcome. It reconstructs only the saved frozen emission vocabulary
and uses native `live_nodes`, `live_text` and `lift_event` on the raw before/after
pages. New values may be read from those pages under the unchanged emission
lifting rule; the saved vocabulary itself does not learn from them.

For decision-list predictions, an undetermined or unnamed answer is
unestablished; an ordinary event is correct exactly when its frame matches the
standing post-action live-region frame. This also applies when the region is
unchanged. Native SILENT is correct exactly when the live-region text is
unchanged. An unavailable channel, missing model or prediction exception keeps
its own unestablished category. Failed primitives are retained independently.

For RULE and LIST separately, an empty admissible set is unestablished. One
event is forced correct or forced wrong according to the same frame/SILENT
comparison, except that a sole-observed-vocabulary Vouch remains unestablished
with sole-correct/sole-wrong recorded separately. Multiple admissible events
remain ambiguous, with whether any matches the observed frame/SILENT recorded.
An unchanged live region does not corroborate every arbitrary event. This is
an explicit measurement policy; it does not call or modify the retained
retrospective scorer's broader unchanged-region shortcut.

Literal output arguments are checked separately from event frames. Preserve
native event/position argument maps and the complete stored argument-role maps.
An exact literal match or mismatch is reported at each comparable position.
Missing bindings, absent positions and native FRESH values keep explicit
unavailable categories; this scorer does not infer a created identity or
freshness from the desired answer. Frame success cannot conceal wrong endpoint
arguments. Raw primitive argument/selection fidelity belongs to a separate
evaluator control.

Report all charged opportunities, all clicks and the fixed 24 target attempts
per split, without pooling setup success into target accuracy. Retain categories
for forced correct, forced wrong, ambiguous and unestablished with denominators.
Report one-witness, two-witness and no-witness subsets only after preserving
the first pass and opening the fixed evaluator labels. Preserve all native
Vouches and complete fitting evidence masks/vocabulary; representative Vouches
are not an enumeration of every surviving conjunction. Event vocabulary is
open: a newly observed frame remains visible even if no candidate named it.

## Diagnosis and acceptance

Preserve all raw inputs/outputs, commitments, predicted bytes and receipts,
complete/partial traces and projections, checkpoints, scores, logs and owned
process records before semantic diagnosis or any learner repair. A complete
inventory is required even for an unfavorable result. Instrument failures
receive an invalid/partial measurement disposition and preserve all available
artifacts; a corrected version cannot overwrite their first-pass identity.

The evaluator independently checks visibility of source, selected target and
the complete bridge alternatives; actual rewiring and selected values; target
flag changes; raw primitive custody; inferred types, keys, references, owner,
selection and output arguments. Missing visibility is unobserved, not evidence
for absence of a relational path. Missing exact-name corroboration alone is
unavailable, not proof of an incorrect unnamed representation.

Outcome accuracy alone establishes neither identity nor relational composition.
The live first pass retains pre-state representations and full training
transition/lift/grounding evidence. State-consequence correctness and rival
identity identification need their own controls. Where multiple identity arms
are compared, use the existing shared-node/slot scoreboard with shared and union
surface sizes, coverage, contradictions and unshared material. No identity
agreement is inferred from an empty surface or a single fitted reading.

Accept a strong generated-fixture composition result only when the learned
condition and bindings account for all rewiring contrasts, held-out combinations
and invariance targets, with independent state/argument corroboration. Otherwise
report the observed categories and the narrowest demonstrated failed layer.
Correctly preserving ambiguity is separate from resolving it. A failed bounded
search does not prove observational equivalence. The first result is complete
when its exposure, custody, controls and failure interpretation are reproducible
and independently reviewed. Then select and execute the smallest justified
general development change on disclosed evidence and reserve a new assessment.
