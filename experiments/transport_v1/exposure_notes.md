# Exposure and design record

The author was a separate fixture-writing agent. Before designing the applications,
it read only the task, the project dependency metadata, and directory names. It did
not read prior fixture application source or retained outcomes. After the public
contract and application semantics were written, it inspected the new generic
`scripts/transport_collect.py` recorder and the public Browser and Observation
interfaces to audit actual action resolution. It did not fit a learner or inspect
learner evaluation outputs.

The two public workflows use card/detail editing and a nested registry with a
separate table/radio chooser. Their ordered numeric operands belong to distinct
visible objects. Initial traces vary both operands and include both successful
and refused ordinary operations. Held-out cases challenge cross-object order,
constant/conjunctive alternatives, and monotone changes in a relevant quantity.
Review operations additionally provide a restricted-observability ambiguity.
Exact cases, rules and outcomes are recorded only under `oracle/`.

A third application uses a structurally distinct workflow. Its contract, initial
trace and withheld cases are frozen now and reserved for post-repair validation.
The author audits its self-consistency, but the root learner/evaluator must not
open its contract, source, cases or trace before the repair checkpoint.

Independence is limited: these are fresh synthetic applications authored by a
separate agent, not independently authored real applications. The application and
reference oracle were produced in one authoring session. Separate implementations
and agreement checks establish self-consistency, not external correctness or
real-world transfer. No application copies, renamed Harbour workflows, or prior
outcome tables were used.

The complete suite and independent oracle specification are written before any
browser outcome audit. The audit manifest is recorded before the audit runs.
The audit is explicitly separate from SemABI evaluation and trains no model.

Revision V1 passed application/oracle self-consistency but failed the requested
full reachable-interface ambiguity guarantee: its review revealed a concealed
bit. The root was told that abstract defect, without withheld values or outcomes,
and authorized a V2 correction before any learner evaluation. V1 source, original
manifest and completed audit are retained intact under `oracle/revisions/v1`.
V2 gives review operands a visible, read-only presentation and uses two distinct
semantic rules that agree throughout the reachable interface. The sealed
specification includes the complete transition argument. Ordinary demand traces
and ordinary evaluation combinations remain unchanged.

Clock-attack eligibility is reported separately. The published dispatch script
has two same-job demand trajectories with at least two rises and no falls.
The workshop script has no trajectory meeting that criterion. These are raw
public-field facts, not proof that SemABI recovered the intended tracked numeric
attribute. A clock-attack claim requires that additional representation check.
